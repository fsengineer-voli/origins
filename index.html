About Origins

Most business ideas don't fail in the market. They fail at the origin.

Origins goes back to where every business starts: the idea, the thinking behind it, and the discipline that turns instinct into a concept people will pay for.

Over two practical days, you work on your own idea or business, with live exercises and direct feedback from Leke Alder.

Whether you have a business already or just an idea you can't shake.

What you leave with

By the end of the workshop, you'll have:

A tested business idea

Your idea run through the laws that govern successful ideas, with the weak points named out loud.

A sharp value proposition

One clear statement of what you do, for whom, and why they should care.

A defined market and customer

The niche you'll win first and a customer persona grounded in real needs.

A brand foundation

Naming principles, brand fundamentals and the thinking behind the business, not just a logo.

A pitch outline

The structure of a business plan and pitch, and what investors actually look for.

Direct feedback from Leke Alder

Frank, practical critique of your idea during the hands-on exercises.

The Facilitator

Leke Alder

He is credited with introducing branding as a discipline to Nigeria, and has advised on policy, politics and business at the highest levels at home and abroad. Origins distils that experience into a working method you can apply to your own idea.

Do I need to have a business to attend?

No. Origins is for people with an existing business and for those still at the idea stage. The early topics cover how ideas are generated, so you can arrive with a running business, a rough idea, or just the urge to build something.

How does the 2-in-1 Bundle work?

It's two tickets for two people, with 10% off the combined price. Both can attend in-person, both online, or one of each. You'll enter both attendees' details when you register.

Is the online experience the same as in-person?

You get the same eight topics, live, with the same facilitator. Online participants join facilitated breakout rooms and can submit their ideas for feedback through the host. In-person attendees also get meals and in-room networking.
